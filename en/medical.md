## What the App is

The App is purely a tool to **help you keep your own records** — essentially a paper notebook — for:

- Past injection dates, doses and sites
- Past weight changes
- Past progress photos

**Every figure is entered, recorded and reviewed by you. The App only organises and displays it.**

---

## About reminders

The weekly reminder is, in essence, **an alarm you set yourself**:

- You choose the reminder time
- A reminder does not mean you **must** inject at that time
- The App is not responsible if a reminder does not fire, is delayed or is not received
- **Follow your prescription and your doctor's instructions for actual injection timing**

---

## About site-rotation hints

The "past injection sites" icons on the new-record screen only **visualise your own history** to help you look back. They are not medical advice:

- Ask your doctor or pharmacist for medical guidance on site rotation
- The App gives no warnings or assessments about "last used" or "not used for a while" sites
- The icons are display-only, not a basis for decisions

## About tirzepatide (Mounjaro / Zepbound)

The drug names mentioned in the App's name and description:

- Only describe what the App is meant to log (descriptive use)
- Do not imply any partnership, licence, endorsement or sponsorship by Eli Lilly and Company or any pharmaceutical company
- **Do not mean the App recommends, promotes or encourages the use of any prescription medication**

Tirzepatide is a prescription medicine that involves:

- Assessing whether it is appropriate for you
- Dose titration (stepwise increases, e.g. 2.5 mg → 5 mg → 7.5 mg → 10 mg → 12.5 mg → 15 mg)
- Monitoring side effects (nausea, diarrhoea, pancreatitis risk, etc.)
- Screening for contraindications (history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, MEN2, etc.)

All of these medical judgements **must be made by your doctor**. The App plays no role in them.
